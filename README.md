# Lance Williams

I build AI systems for small businesses, ministries, and nonprofits. The AI does the busywork. A person approves anything that goes out.

I came to this from field work, building relationships with leaders across Oklahoma, Arkansas, and Missouri. That's where I learned what slows a small team down. Now I take the work a team does by hand and turn it into something that runs on its own, built with Claude Code.

## What I build

- **Watchers and rankers.** Agents that read job boards, photo libraries, or lead lists, score what they find, and send only the best to your phone.
- **Approval-first automation.** Nothing is sent, posted, or paid for without a yes from a person.
- **Plain-language handoff.** Every project ships with setup steps a non-technical owner can follow.

## Projects

- **[ai-job-ranker](https://github.com/lw9841-coder/ai-job-ranker)**: watches 84 companies' job boards, has Claude score each posting against a written profile, and sends the best matches to my phone.
- **[instagram-photo-poster](https://github.com/lw9841-coder/instagram-photo-poster)**: finds the best photos in Apple Photos, briefs me for the caption, and posts to Instagram only after I approve.
- **[purchase-review-skill](https://github.com/lw9841-coder/purchase-review-skill)**: a Claude skill that reviews a shopping cart, gives budget, value, and premium picks with one clear choice, checks its own work, and never buys. Tested with written evals.
